# Style guidelines: exercise sheet

- E1 [style] Each exercise can be read alone: its hypotheses and notation are in it or in a short reminder at the top.
- E2 [style] Questions are numbered, each asks for one thing, and the difficulty increases.
- E3 [style] Hints are set apart from the questions.
- E4 [style] Solutions, when asked for, are in their own section, in the notation of the exercise, complete, and each
  refers to its exercise with `\ref`.
- E5 [style] A solution uses only the course material and earlier questions, and says which.
