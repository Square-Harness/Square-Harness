# Style guidelines: exam

- X1 [required] Header. Course, date, duration and the instructions (documents, calculators) are at the top.
- X2 [required] Points. Every question carries its points; the points of an exercise add up to its total, and the totals to
  the total of the exam.
- X3 [style] Independence. The text says which exercises are independent; a question that needs an earlier one says
  which, and gives the earlier result so that it can be admitted.
- X4 [style] Questions. Each question asks for one thing, with an unambiguous verb (prove, compute, state, deduce).
- X5 [required] Notation. The notation and the results that may be used without proof are recalled before the exercises.
- X6 [style] No solution and no hint beyond what the request asks for.
- X7 [style] Feasibility. The length fits the duration; no question needs a result outside the course material.
