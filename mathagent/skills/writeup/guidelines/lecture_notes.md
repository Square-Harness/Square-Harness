# Style guidelines: lecture notes

- L1 [style] Each section starts with what it is for, before the definitions.
- L2 [style] Each definition is followed by an example, and where useful a non-example.
- L3 [style] Statements are proved, or the text says where the proof is and why it is left out.
- L4 [style] Remarks point out the usual mistakes and what each hypothesis is for.
- L5 [style] The rules P1 to P8 of the paper guidelines apply.
